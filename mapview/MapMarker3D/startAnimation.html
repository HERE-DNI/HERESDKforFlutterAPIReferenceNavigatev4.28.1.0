void startAnimation(
  1. MapMarkerAnimation animation,

Starts animation of this 3D marker according to provided MapMarkerAnimation.

Supports tracks created with MapItemKeyFrameTrack 'moveTo', 'scale' and 'opacity' methods.

Any ongoing animation of this marker instance will get cancelled.

  • animation The animation to start, may be used for multiple different 3D markers.

  • animationListener The listener to receive notifications about animation start, completion or cancellation.
